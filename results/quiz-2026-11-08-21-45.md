## Missed questions

1. **Regularity messages are messages...** _(40-069, Message priority)_  
   - Your answer: sent by an aircraft operating agency or an aircraft of immediate concern to an aircraft in flight.  
   - Why wrong: That describes an operational message, not the defined flight-regularity category.  
   - Correct: concerning the operation or maintenance of facilities essential for the safety or regularity of aircraft operation. — Flight-regularity messages concern operation or maintenance of facilities essential to safe and regular flight.
2. **What is a cause for the dip error on the direct-reading compass?** _(80-123, Magnetic compass)_  
   - Your answer: Acceleration of the airplane  
   - Why wrong: Acceleration produces compass error, but dip is caused by magnetic-field inclination.  
   - Correct: Inclination of earth's magnetic field lines — Dip error results from inclination of Earth's magnetic field lines to the horizontal.
3. **If surrounded by airflow (v>0), any arbitrarily shaped body produces...** _(51-003, Pressure and airflow)_  
   - Your answer: drag and lift.  
   - Why wrong: Not every arbitrary body or orientation produces lift, although it does produce drag.  
   - Correct: drag. — Drag is the unavoidable aerodynamic force on a body moving through air.
4. **The speed Vy is defined as...** _(70-077, Climb & cruise performance)_  
   - Your answer: best speed of climb.  
   - Why wrong: Vy is not simply “best speed” without specifying the climb objective.  
   - Correct: best rate of climb. — Vy is the speed for best rate of climb.
   - Remember: Vy maximizes altitude gained per unit time.
5. **Which statement about the airflow around an aerofoil is correct if the angle of attack decreases?** _(51-060, Lift and centre of pressure)_  
   - Your answer: The center of pressure moves forward  
   - Why wrong: Forward CP movement corresponds to increasing, not decreasing, AoA.  
   - Correct: The center of pressure moves aft — As AoA decreases on the cambered profile, the centre of pressure moves aft.

   - Why wrong: Human mistakes are not the specific reason this item associates with complacency.
