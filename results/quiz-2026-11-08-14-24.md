## Missed questions

1. **What information is NOT found on Low-Level Significant Weather Charts (LLSWC)?** _(30-138, Weather Charts and Imagery)_  
   - Your answer: Information about icing conditions  
   - Why wrong: LLSWC includes icing forecasts and areas.  
   - Correct: Radar echos of precipitation — Radar echoes are not plotted as precipitation images on an LLSWC.
2. **How does the aircraft configuration influence take-off performance while all other parameters remaining constant?** _(70-019, Take-off & landing performance)_  
   - Your answer: Aircraft A has a higher tyre pressure than aircraft B  
   - Why wrong: Higher tyre pressure reduces rolling resistance and would tend to improve, not worsen, take-off roll.  
   - Correct: Aircraft A has a higher flap setting than aircraft B — A higher take-off flap setting generally shortens ground roll.
   - Remember: More flap can shorten ground run but reduces climb performance.
3. **SIGMET warnings are issued for...** _(30-103, Aviation Weather Reports)_  
   - Your answer: airports.  
   - Why wrong: Airport weather is reported by METAR/TAF and ATIS, not SIGMET scope.  
   - Correct: FIRs / UIRs. — SIGMET warnings are issued for flight information regions or upper information regions.
4. **What information is provided in the general part (GEN) of the AIP?** _(10-042, AIP, NOTAM, PIB and restricted airspace)_  
   - Your answer: Access restrictions for airfields, passenger controls, requirements for pilots, license samples and validity periods  
   - Why wrong: Pilot licensing and access rules are not the GEN items listed by the bank.  
   - Correct: Map icons, list of radio nav aids, time for sunrise and sunset, airport fees, air traffic control fees — GEN includes general material such as symbols, navigation aids, times, and charges.
5. **What information from a ground station does not require readback?** _(40-046, Readback)_  
   - Your answer: Taxi instructions  
   - Why wrong: Taxi instructions must be read back to confirm the route and hold instructions.  
   - Correct: Traffic information — Traffic information does not require readback.
6. **Which statement describes a situation of static stability?** _(51-078, Stability and centre of gravity)_  
   - Your answer: An aircraft distorted by external impact will maintain the deflected position  
   - Why wrong: Remaining in the displaced position describes neutral, not positive, static stability.  
   - Correct: An aircraft distorted by external impact will return to the original position — Positive static stability means an initial tendency to return toward the original condition after disturbance.
   - Remember: Static stability is the initial tendency; dynamic stability describes what follows over time.
7. **The term "runway" is defined as a...** _(10-046, Aerodrome operations, signs, markings and lights)_  
   - Your answer: rectangular area on a land or water aerodrome prepared for the landing and take-off of aircraft.  
   - Why wrong: The standard runway definition is on a land aerodrome, not a water aerodrome.  
   - Correct: rectangular area on a land aerodrome prepared for the landing and take-off of aircraft. — A runway is a rectangular area on a land aerodrome prepared for aircraft take-off and landing.
