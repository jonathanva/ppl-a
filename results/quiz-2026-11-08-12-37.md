## Missed questions

1. **During an approach the aeroplane experiences a windshear with a decreasing tailwind. If the pilot does not make any corrections, how do the approach path and the indicated airspeed (IAS) change?** _(60-018, Wind shear and microbursts)_  
   - Your answer: Path is higher, IAS decreases  
   - Why wrong: A decreasing tailwind is equivalent to an increasing headwind, so IAS rises rather than falls.  
   - Correct: Path is higher, IAS increases — A decreasing tailwind acts like increasing headwind, raising IAS and the approach path.
   - Remember: A tailwind decrease is a headwind increase.
2. **In which way does the position of the center of pressure move at a positively shaped profile with increasing angle of attack?** _(51-057, Lift and centre of pressure)_  
   - Your answer: It moves backward until reaching the critical angle of attack  
   - Why wrong: With increasing AoA, CP moves forward up to the critical angle, not backward.  
   - Correct: It moves forward until reaching the critical angle of attack — On the positively cambered profile, CP moves forward as AoA increases until critical AoA.
   - Remember: Near and beyond stall, pressure changes can cause an abrupt aft movement.
3. **Ground-based weather radar is used to detect and display:** _(30-089, Weather Charts and Imagery)_  
   - Your answer: Clouds  
   - Why wrong: Radar detects hydrometeor echoes, not clouds as a complete field.  
   - Correct: Precipitation — Weather radar detects and displays precipitation.
4. **What leads to a decreased stall speed Vs (IAS)?** _(51-024, Stall and recovery)_  
   - Your answer: Lower density  
   - Why wrong: Lower density mainly raises true stall speed; it does not lower indicated stall speed in this comparison.  
   - Correct: Decreasing weight — Lower weight means less lift is required, so the aircraft can stall at a lower IAS.
   - Remember: For the same configuration, Vs varies approximately with the square root of weight.
5. **The rotational axis of the Earth runs through the...** _(90-002, Earth, Coordinates and Distances)_  
   - Your answer: magnetic north pole and on the magnetic south pole.  
   - Why wrong: magnetic north pole and… conflicts with the rule: Earth’s rotational axis passes through the geographic poles, not the magnetic poles.  
   - Correct: geographic North Pole and on the geographic South Pole. — Earth’s rotational axis passes through the geographic poles, not the magnetic poles.
6. **The term 'civil twilight' is defined as...** _(90-018, Time, UTC, Longitude and Sun)_  
   - Your answer: the period of time before sunrise or after sunset where the midpoint of the sun disk is 6 degrees or less below the apparent horizon.  
   - Why wrong: the period of time… conflicts with the rule: Civil twilight is when the Sun’s centre is within 6° below the true horizon.  
   - Correct: the period of time before sunrise or after sunset where the midpoint of the sun disk is 6 degrees or less below the true horizon. — Civil twilight is when the Sun’s centre is within 6° below the true horizon.
