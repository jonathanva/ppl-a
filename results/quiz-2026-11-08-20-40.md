2. **Compared to trailing edge flaps, leading edge devices like Slots...** _(51-031, High-lift devices and wing design)_  
   - Your answer: reduce the critical angle of attack at a given speed.  
   - Why wrong: Slots increase the usable critical angle; they do not reduce it.  
   - Correct: produce less drag while allowing a higher angle of attack. — Compared with trailing-edge flaps, slots produce less drag while delaying separation to permit higher AoA.
   - Remember: Flaps increase camber; slots/slats delay separation and extend usable AoA.
3. **What can be said about the load factor (n) and the stall speed (Vs) in a co-ordinated turn?** _(51-025, Turns and load factor)_  
   - Your answer: n is larger than 1, Vs is lower than in straight and level flight.  
   - Why wrong: Higher load factor raises stall speed; it does not lower it.  
   - Correct: n is larger than 1, Vs is higher than in straight and level flight. — A level coordinated turn requires greater lift, so n > 1 and Vs is higher.
   - Remember: Vs in a turn = wings-level Vs × √n.
