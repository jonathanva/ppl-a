1. **How do dew point and relative humidity change with decreasing temperature?** _(30-118, Humidity and Stability)_  
   - Your answer: Dew point remains constant, relative humidity decreases  
   - Why wrong: Cooling with unchanged moisture leaves dew point approximately constant and raises relative humidity.  
   - Correct: Dew point remains constant, relative humidity increases — This is correct: temperature falls toward the roughly constant dew point, increasing RH.
2. **What is the parallax error?** _(20-013, Nervous system, senses and vision)_  
   - Your answer: Long-sightedness due to aging especially at night  
   - Why wrong: Age-related long-sightedness is a focusing condition, not an off-axis instrument-reading error.  
   - Correct: Wrong interpretation of instruments caused by the angle of vision — Parallax is an instrument-reading error from viewing a scale at an angle.

   - Why wrong: Five kilometres is the lower altitude-band minimum.  
   - Correct: 8000 m — Above FL100, the bank minimum is 8 km.
4. **How should a power decrease be executed on a constant-speed propeller, provided that no other procedure is described in the flight manual?** _(80-114, Oil, cooling, engine instruments and propeller)_  
   - Your answer: 1) Decrease RPM 2) Increase manifold pressure  
   - Why wrong: The bank sequence reduces manifold pressure first, then RPM; this option reverses it.  
   - Correct: 1) Decrease manifold pressure 2) Decrease RPM — Power decrease sequence is throttle/MAP down first, followed by RPM down.
5. **The VFR semicircular rules are based on the...** _(70-095, Wind, GS, time)_  
   - Your answer: magnetic heading (MH).  
   - Why wrong: Magnetic heading includes wind correction; the rule uses course.  
   - Correct: magnetic course (MC). — VFR semicircular rules use magnetic course.
   - Remember: Convert to magnetic course, then apply the VFR level rule.
6. **Baggage and cargo must be properly stowed and fastened, otherwise a shift of the cargo may cause...** _(70-063, Mass & balance)_  
   - Your answer: calculable instability if the C.G. is shifting by less than 10 %.  
   - Why wrong: A shifted load may create uncontrollable attitudes, structural damage, and injury risk.  
   - Correct: uncontrollable attitudes, structural damage, risk of injuries. — The consequences can be serious even with a small shift; this is not safely calculable under a 10% threshold.
   - Remember: Restrain cargo against movement in all expected manoeuvres.
7. **In airspace "D" a Boeing 737 and a Cessna 152 are flying on crossing courses. Which aeroplane has to divert?** _(10-074, Right of way and collision avoidance)_

   - Why wrong: ATC may provide instructions, but the bank’s tested crossing rule uses relative position.  
   - Correct: The aeroplane flying from right to left has priority, the other one has to divert — The aircraft with the other on its right has priority; the other gives way.
8. **Wake turbulences develop during take-off just as the aeroplane...** _(60-022, Wake turbulence and jet blast)_  
   - Your answer: accelerates.  
   - Why wrong: Acceleration alone does not mark onset; wake starts when the lifting aircraft becomes airborne at nosewheel lift-off.  
   - Correct: lifts off with the front gear. — The bank’s take-off reference is lift-off with the front gear, when the aircraft begins generating its airborne wake.
   - Remember: Wake begins at lift-off; do not assume it starts at a particular height.
