1. **Wake turbulences develop during take-off just as the aeroplane...** _(60-022, Wake turbulence and jet blast)_  
   - Your answer: lifts off with the main gear.  
   - Why wrong: Waiting for main-gear lift-off is later than the keyed nosewheel-lift-off point.  
   - Correct: lifts off with the front gear. — The bank’s take-off reference is lift-off with the front gear, when the aircraft begins generating its airborne wake.
   - Remember: Wake begins at lift-off; do not assume it starts at a particular height.
2. **How should a power decrease be executed on a constant-speed propeller, provided that no other procedure is described in the flight manual?** _(80-114, Oil, cooling, engine instruments and propeller)_  
   - Your answer: 1) Decrease manifold pressure 2) Increase RPM  
   - Why wrong: Increasing RPM while reducing power is not the required two-step sequence.  
   - Correct: 1) Decrease manifold pressure 2) Decrease RPM — Power decrease sequence is throttle/MAP down first, followed by RPM down.
