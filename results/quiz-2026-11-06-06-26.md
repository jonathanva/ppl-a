## Missed questions

1. **A pilot wants to take off on runway 36, the reported wind is 240 degrees, 12 knots. What is the value of the wind components acting on the aircraft on take-off and landing?** _(70-081, Wind, GS, time)_  
   - Your answer: Crosswind from the right 10.4 kt. Tailwind 6 kt.  
   - Why wrong: The crosswind magnitude is 10.4 kt, but it comes from the left and the longitudinal component is tailwind.  
   - Correct: Crosswind from the left 10.4 kt. Tailwind 6 kt. — Runway 360 versus wind 240 gives 120° difference: 12 sin60° = 10.4 kt from left and 12 cos60° = 6 kt tailwind.
   - Remember: Wind direction is FROM; resolve with sine for crosswind and cosine for longitudinal.
2. **What factors are required for the formation of precipitation in clouds?** _(30-042, Clouds and Precipitation)_  
   - Your answer: High humidity and high temperatures  
   - Why wrong: Humidity and warmth alone do not ensure particles grow large enough to fall.  
   - Correct: Moderate to strong updrafts — Moderate or strong updrafts support droplet/ice-particle growth to precipitation size.
3. **The distance between two airports is 220 NM. On an aeronautical navigation chart the pilot measures 40.7 cm for this distance. The chart scale is...** _(90-035, Units and Chart Scale)_  
   - Your answer: 1 : 250000.  
   - Why wrong: 1 : 250000. conflicts with the rule: 220 NM is about 40,744,000 cm; ÷40.7 cm gives scale 1:1,000,000.  
   - Correct: 1 : 1000000. — 220 NM is about 40,744,000 cm; ÷40.7 cm gives scale 1:1,000,000.
